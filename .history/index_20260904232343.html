METAFRENS

Metafrens is a curated, close-knit, free-access community of Web3 enthusiasts united by a shared passion for NFTs, Ordinals, and DeFi.
We’re focused on providing quality Web3 education, a supportive environment where members can learn, grow, connect, and access top-tier alphas and opportunities across the Web3 space.

OUR JOURNEY

METAFRENS was officially founded in September 2022, and since then, we’ve evolved into a vibrant community of Web3 enthusiasts united by a shared passion for NFTs, Ordinals, and DeFi.
We’re dedicated to exploring the latest trends in Web3, fostering fun and insightful conversations, and creating a space where members can learn, grow, and build meaningful connections.
Since our inception, we’ve hosted several onboarding sessions across our private Discord and X Spaces, helping both our members and wider audience stay informed and better understand the latest developments in the ever-evolving Web3 space.

METAFRENS WEEKLY EVENTS

Alpha Nights

Our weekly alpha nights are always packed with fresh alphas! It's a time where we come together as a community to share the latest alphas, uncover hidden gems, discuss emerging trends, and stay one step ahead in the ever-evolving Web3 space.

Beyond the alphas, it's also a great opportunity to connect, build relationships, and strengthen our community bonds.

Every Friday · 8:00 PM UTC

Metafrens Hangout

Metafrens Hangout X Spaces are the perfect way to kick off the week. It's a chance for us to unwind, have fun, connect with frens, and, of course, share some alpha.

Beyond the vibes, we host engaging conversations around trending developments in the Web3 space, educational sessions to help our audience and members stay informed about new developments, including AMA sessions with builders and industry leaders.

Our Hangout Spaces also give our wider audience a glimpse into what makes Metafrens Familia unique, including our culture, conversations, and community.

Every Sunday · 8:00 PM UTC
